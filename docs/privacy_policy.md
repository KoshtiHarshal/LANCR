# Privacy Policy for LANCR

**Last updated: 20 June 2026**

LANCR ("we", "our", or "us") operates the LANCR mobile application (the "App"),
a marketplace that connects clients and freelancers. This Privacy Policy
explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using LANCR, you agree to the collection and use of information in line with
this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account & profile information.** When you create an account we collect your
email address and the profile details you provide — such as your name, role
(client or freelancer), headline, bio, location, company, skills, experience,
profile photo, and portfolio items.

**Content you create.** Projects you post, proposals you submit, messages you
send, and reviews and ratings you leave or receive.

**Usage & device data.** Basic technical data needed to operate the App,
including a push-notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to
deliver notifications to your device.

We do **not** collect payment card details — LANCR does not currently process
payments in-app.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To create and manage your account and profile.
- To operate core features: posting projects, submitting and reviewing
  proposals, messaging between matched users, and ratings/reviews.
- To send you in-app and push notifications about activity relevant to you
  (new proposals, acceptances, messages, project completion).
- To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

## 3. How Your Information Is Shared

- **With other users**, as part of the service: your public profile, projects,
  proposals, reviews, and messages are visible to the users you interact with
  (for example, a client sees proposals on their project; a freelancer sees the
  client of a project they applied to). Profiles and reviews are publicly
  viewable within the App.
- **With service providers** that host and power the App:
  - **Supabase** — database, authentication, storage, and realtime services.
  - **Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging)** — push notification delivery.
- **For legal reasons**, if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure and protected with row-level
security so that users can only access the data they are permitted to see.
While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of
transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

- **Access and update**: edit your profile and content from within the App.
- **Notifications**: turn push notifications off in Settings.
- **Delete your account**: use Settings → Delete account. This permanently
  removes your account and associated personal data.

## 6. Children's Privacy

LANCR is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your
country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be
reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
