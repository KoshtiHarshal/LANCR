# Delete your LANCR account & data

LANCR lets you permanently delete your account and associated data at any time —
directly inside the app, with no need to contact us.

## How to delete your account (in-app)
1. Open the **LANCR** app and sign in.
2. Tap your **profile avatar** (top-right of the home screen) to open your profile.
3. Tap the **Settings** (gear) icon.
4. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**.
5. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

## What gets deleted
Deleting your account permanently removes:
- Your **profile** (name, headline, bio, location, company, avatar, skills, portfolio).
- Your **projects**, **proposals**, **messages**, **reviews**, **saved projects**, and **notifications**.
- Your **authentication record**.

This action is **immediate and irreversible** — deleted data cannot be recovered.

## Data retention
We do not retain personal data after deletion, except where required by law
(for example, limited transactional records). Aggregated, non-identifying data
may be retained.

## Need help?
If you cannot access the app and want your account and data deleted, email us at
**lancrapp0@gmail.com** from the address associated with your account and we will
process the deletion.
